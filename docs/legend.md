## Overview
MX-01 operates via reactive distortion sampling within a structured optical field.  
The system identifies spatial irregularities by evaluating anisotropic deviations across projected lattice geometries, allowing for adaptive maneuvering through unknown or dynamically shifting environments.

---

## System Layer
- **Core Environment**: MXOS Runtime v1.0
- **Protocol Stack**: GVD-NAV (Grid Vector Displacement – Navigation)
- **Execution Platform**: Embedded real-time microcontroller (ESP-class)
- **Input Structure**: Multi-lateral optical phase deformation arrays (LPC-S2)

---

## Capabilities
- Dynamic vector reorientation from surface inconsistency detection
- Topology-agnostic path modulation under zero-prior conditions
- No reliance on semantic classification or external positioning signals
- Stability in degraded visual domains (fog, smoke, low-light)
